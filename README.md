# Sharova Business Intelligence

Sharova Business Intelligence is a private decision dashboard for small businesses.

## What it does

Record sales, expenses, stock and customer balances, then use Sharova's business signals to understand:

- What happened today
- What changed
- What is unusual
- What matters
- What to do next

## Customer access

Customers receive a unique activation code with their purchase. They open the Sharova web app, enter their purchase email and activation code, and activate the product on their device.

Keep activation codes private. Each purchase should receive its own code.

## Customer reminders

The Customer Money area can save a customer's phone number and prepare a friendly balance reminder. Sending the reminder opens the device SMS composer with the message ready for review; Sharova does not send SMS automatically in the background.

## Current product model

Sharova is delivered as a web application. Business records in the current version are stored locally on the customer's device.
